# Community conduct

Xcaliber welcomes good-faith users and contributors across experience levels.

Be direct about evidence, respectful toward people, and specific about code. Do not
harass, threaten, demean, dox, or discriminate. Do not pressure anyone to publish
private prompts, credentials, model files, hardware identifiers, or other sensitive
data. Performance and model-correctness claims must remain reproducible and may be
challenged on technical grounds without attacking the person making them.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when these rules are violated. Report conduct or security
concerns privately through the repository's Security Advisory form.

This policy applies in the repository, issue tracker, reviews, discussions, and other
spaces represented as part of the Xcaliber project.
